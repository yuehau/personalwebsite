📋 How to Print & Use

  1. Publish all your files (index.html, card.html, chinyuehau.vcf) free on GitHub Pages — see the deploy guide
  2. Paste your published site URL above and click Update QR (the QR auto-points to your Digital Card)
  3. Program the NFC chip with the same Digital Card URL using the free NFC Tools app
  4. Press Ctrl+P (⌘+P on Mac) → margins None, enable background graphics
  5. Print on 350gsm matte/glossy stock, size 90mm × 54mm — trim if needed
💡 How it works: tapping the NFC card (or scanning the QR) opens your Digital Card, where visitors hit Save My Contact & WhatsApp Me in one tap. Both QR codes auto-point to card.html on the URL you set above.
